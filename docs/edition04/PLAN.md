# Edition 04 — Held lives on the page

## Accepted corrections and new ideas

Oscar clarified that simply visiting must lend a small amount of **actual model-layer computation**, without a click; coffee adds more. The previous automatic sampling-only flow did not satisfy this. The public model must run from the Mac mini during launch while browser capacity grows. A separate studio alone did not satisfy this.

New visual direction: remove the analog Tamagotchi shell. Held lives in the page and moves among a computer for plans/delegation, a drawing table, a reading/memory corner and a quiet place. Personify actual agent interactions. A free coffee temporarily boosts this tab's compute. Explain the entire idea immediately. Expose real presence, helper jobs, progress and source of power, with more detail available on demand. Keep every test headless; visible Chrome windows disrupted Oscar's desktop.

## Chosen behavior

- New visible visits automatically load a gentle two-layer piece at a 5% measured work/rest target, with immediate disclosure and Pause/watch-only. Respect saved opt-out and data-saving preference. Unsupported devices stay welcome and may do bounded CPU work. No promise every device can hold a layer.
- A free coffee temporarily selects a 10% work/rest target and up to four layers for ten minutes. No payment or application installation. Model data transfers, battery/energy use and hardware variability are explained honestly. Stop remains one action.
- One public habitat: a native MLX copy of the same pinned SmolLM2 quantization supports launch. Complete browser chains receive jobs first; when none is complete, the Mini can keep the launch running. The UI exposes this source. Once browser independence is deliberately enabled through an authenticated operator command, no Mini fallback is allowed. This prevents test traffic from prematurely retiring launch support.
- An open illustrated workspace with Held and helper sprites positioned by real job kinds. Idle wandering is decorative and labeled; no fictional helper counts or fabricated work. Users can inspect stations, public drafts, completed files and exact memory trials.
- Live numbers distinguish visible tabs, ready layer holders, complete chains and actively running helpers. No private identities/IPs, secrets or visitor device fingerprints are exposed.

## Limits retained

Configured eight chains and 300 connections are not proof of large-scale capacity. Model quality is weak. Browser results can be forged. Work/rest budgets are not hardware utilization or energy caps; loading costs are additional. Saved model weights and artwork remain after thought generation pauses. More helper copies do not train or enlarge the underlying model.
